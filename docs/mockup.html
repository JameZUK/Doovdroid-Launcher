Doovdroid Launcher

The home screen we're building for the Doov phone. It's one screen, worked entirely with the keys. Press the keys under the screen to try it, or use your keyboard. Tapping the screen does nothing here, the same as on the phone.

The keys

▲ ▼
Move the highlight up and down the list. Nothing scrolls; everything is on the one screen.
OK
Call the highlighted person, or open the highlighted item.
Left –
Does what the bar at the bottom of the screen says above it: Call, Open or Select. It also unlocks the lock screen.
Right –
Does what the bar says above it: Delete a digit while dialling (hold it to clear the number), Close in the admin menu. Inside other apps it goes back.
Green
Calls the highlighted person, or the number you've typed.
0–9
Start typing a phone number. Hold 0 for +.
* #
Volume down and up. That's built into the phone, so they can't be dialled from this screen.
Red
This is also the power key. It hangs up a call. Otherwise it turns the screen off, and waking the phone always comes back to this screen. Hold it for the power menu.

Try it

Screen colours

On a computer: arrow keys, Enter for OK, Backspace for the right dash key, Esc for the red key, and digits to dial.

What it hands over to

  • Calls use the phone's own call screen. Green answers, red hangs up.
  • Messages, WhatsApp, Recent calls and Contacts open the phone's own apps. The right dash key backs out of them, and waking the phone always comes back here.
  • The notification pull-down, Settings and the app store are blocked. Facebook, YouTube, Chrome and Gmail are switched off.
  • Texts are typed with the keypad (T9), not an on-screen keyboard.
  • For you: typing 0000 and then a PIN on this screen opens an admin menu with Settings, unlocked for 15 minutes. No real phone number starts with 0000. This mockup uses 00009999; the real PIN is set on the phone.

Names and counts are examples. On the phone, the six names come from a settings file kept on the phone itself, in the order you choose.
